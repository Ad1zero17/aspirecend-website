2. In brief

Without your consent, this website does not load any services from Google or Meta and does not set any cookies for advertising or analytics purposes. Fonts, images and the interview video are stored on our own web space. Client videos hosted on Vimeo are only loaded when you click on a video.

Only if you consent in the cookie banner do we load Google Tag Manager and the Meta Pixel in order to measure the performance of our ads. You can withdraw your consent at any time via Cookie settings.

3. Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data in order to deliver the page and protect it against attacks: IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, the amount of data transferred, browser type and operating system, and the previously visited page (referrer).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely, quickly and reliably. Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf on the basis of a data processing agreement. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in addition, the standard contractual clauses of the EU Commission apply. Further information: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

4. Consent and cookie settings

On your first visit, our own cookie banner asks whether you agree to the use of marketing services. We store your decision for 12 months in the local storage of your browser (entry “asp-consent”). This entry contains only your choice and the time it was made and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

Storing it is technically necessary in order to respect your decision (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Cookie settings.

5. Google Tag Manager

With your consent, we use Google Tag Manager. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Tag Manager is a tool we use to integrate the Meta Pixel into the website. It does not create user profiles itself and does not analyse your behaviour, but when it loads it records your IP address, which may also be transferred to Google servers in the USA.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Further information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Meta Pixel

With your consent, we use the Meta Pixel. The provider is Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland. We use the pixel to measure how visitors use our website after clicking on one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram, and to show our ads to suitable audiences.

This involves processing, among other things, your IP address, information about your browser and device, the pages you visit and your actions on the website. For this purpose Meta sets cookies (for example “_fbp” and “_fbc”) which are stored for up to 90 days. The data is transferred to Meta, including Meta Platforms, Inc. in the USA. Meta may link the data to your Facebook or Instagram account and use it for its own advertising purposes.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). We are jointly responsible with Meta for collecting the data on this website and transmitting it to Meta (Art. 26 GDPR). The corresponding agreement can be found here: https://www.facebook.com/legal/controller_addendum. Meta is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Further information: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

7. Videos

Vimeo

Our clients’ video testimonials are hosted on Vimeo. The provider is Vimeo.com, Inc., 330 West 34th Street, 5th Floor, New York, NY 10001, USA. The video player is only loaded when you click on a video. Before that, no connection to Vimeo is established. We load the player with the “Do Not Track” setting.

When you start a video, Vimeo receives your IP address and technical information about your browser and device; the data may be transferred to the USA. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by clicking on the video (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Further information: https://vimeo.com/privacy

Videos on our own web space

The interview video on this website is stored on our own web space. No data is transferred to third parties when it is played.

8. Booking appointments via Calendly

For booking calls we link to Calendly. The provider is Calendly LLC, 115 E Main St, Ste A1B, Buford, GA 30518, USA. You only leave our website when you click the link. The data you enter there (for example name, e-mail address, information about your company and the selected time slot) is processed by Calendly on our behalf so that we can hold the appointment with you.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract). The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the EU Commission. Further information: https://calendly.com/privacy

9. Contact by e-mail

If you write to us by e-mail, we process your details in order to answer your inquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your inquiry relates to a contract or its initiation, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete the data as soon as it is no longer required and no statutory retention obligations apply.

10. Online calls

We hold calls with prospective and existing clients by video conference via Zoom or Google Meet. The data required for the call is processed (for example name, e-mail address, audio and video data during the call, and technical connection data). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The provider of Zoom is Zoom Communications Inc., 55 Almaden Boulevard, 6th Floor, San Jose, CA 95113, USA. Further information: https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/privacy/

The provider of Google Meet is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Further information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

With both services, data may be transferred to the USA. This is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the EU Commission.

11. Data transfers to the USA

Some of the providers named above are based in the USA or process data there. For companies certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the EU Commission has determined that an adequate level of data protection exists. Where this does not apply, we base the transfer on the standard contractual clauses of the EU Commission.

12. Storage period

Unless a shorter period is stated in this policy, we store personal data only for as long as is necessary for the respective purpose. Statutory retention periods, for example under commercial and tax law, remain unaffected.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we store about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to the processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise your rights, an e-mail to tobias@aspirecend.com is sufficient. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

14. Encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise this by “https://” in the address bar of your browser.

15. Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy when the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.
