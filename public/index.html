FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

We want to be as transparent as possible. Therefore, you will find the most frequently asked questions below.

Do you only run ads on Facebook and Instagram?

Facebook and Instagram form our basis and will be the biggest growth channel for you. That’s where we start in 9 out of 10 cases. As we scale, we look at other channels we can add. Depending on your market, we often implement Google, TikTok and Pinterest. If you are already running ads on other platforms, we can of course take them over immediately.

Who creates the creatives/visuals for the ads?

We start with your existing ads and then create ads ourselves. All we need from you are your existing raw images/videos. If you have a creative designer on the team, you are welcome to create the ads. In addition, we will advise you and send creative input so that we can continuously optimize the ads together.

How often and with whom do we have contact?

We are available 7 days a week via Slack or WhatsApp and respond very quickly. You can also use our online calendar to schedule a digital meeting with us at any time. You will always have direct contact with Tobias, the CEO & Founder and Giovanni, the CMO.

Who runs the ads?

Tobias and Giovanni run the ads and develop new strategies for continuous optimization and improvement. You will NOT be placed with any media buyer, but will be managed directly by Tobias himself.

What kind of advertising budget do I need to start?

This varies. We usually recommend a monthly budget of €2,000 – €3,000 per month to our customers at the beginning. If you are already running ads, we will maintain the current budgets. The expected returns and profits depend on several factors. In the first meeting, we will go through this in detail using an Excel spreadsheet so that you know exactly when to expect results.

You don’t have as large a team as other agencies, can you manage the work?

We deliberately opt for a small team so that we can concentrate fully on the customers we work with. We focus on a small part of the market, which, in combination with the right systems, allows us to do 110% of the work.

What exactly does the reporting look like? How do we know how well the ads are performing?

You receive a weekly written report on all results. In addition, we have a monthly call in which all figures are clearly presented. In addition, we can inform you individually about results and changes 24/7 on request. This means you always know how the ads are performing and how your budget is being managed.

If we were to decide to work together, what would be the next steps?

You would go through our free onboarding process where we get everything we need, e.g. raw material and images/videos and access to your ad managers. This takes no more than 30 minutes. Then we have an onboarding call to answer any final questions and make sure we have everything we need. Finally, the ads will be live in 3-5 days.
